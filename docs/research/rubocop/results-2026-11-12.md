# rubocop compiled: results 2026-11-12 (branch rubocop-compile, decision 444)

Inputs live in the scratchpad `work/` dir next to the patched gem copies: a.rb (2 lines), c.rb (clean), b.rb
(rubocop's cli.rb), d..n.rb (other rubocop/rubocop-ast sources, 58-793 lines), and `sub/` (a directory of 3 files).
`cmp.sh <tag> files...` compares stdout and exit status with MRI and fails on any "An error occurred while" in the
compiled run's stderr.

## Correctness

The all-cops binary (round f36, every registered cop compiled) equals MRI on all 15 inputs: stdout, exit status, and
no cop errors. Optimized and debug builds agree.

## Run time

`bench.py`: median of 5, MRI / optimized / debug interleaved. The machine was not idle (load average 19 at the
start, 45 at the end), so these are rough; the ratio is what to read.

| input | MRI | compiled, optimized | speedup | compiled, `-N -l` debug |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a.rb (2 lines) | 1.39 s | 0.45 s | 3.1x | 0.67 s |
| m.rb (58 lines) | 1.11 s | 0.43 s | 2.6x | 0.56 s |
| b.rb (250 lines) | 1.25 s | 0.62 s | 2.0x | 1.05 s |
| k.rb (790 lines, 84 offenses) | 2.48 s | 1.81 s | 1.4x | 4.86 s |
| f.rb (578 lines, 175 offenses) | 5.51 s | 3.38 s | 1.6x | 16.41 s |
| sub/ (3 files) | 7.05 s | 1.32 s | 5.3x | 2.13 s |

The debug build turns off the Go optimizer and inlining (`-gcflags=-N -l`) for faster round trips; it is slower than
MRI on the large files.

## Build cost (not met yet)

- Generated Go: 216k functions (182k before every registered cop was compiled; the extra cops are needed because a
  directive comment can name any cop).
- Optimized `go build`: 293 MB binary. The one measured build took 73 min wall clock but 22.6 min user and 49.5 min
  system time: two copies ran at once by mistake and swapped 12 GB. A clean single build still needs measuring.
- Transpile: 10-19 min under today's load; ~4.5 min on a quiet machine before the extra cops.
